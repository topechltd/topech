Application Scenarios

From lubricant showrooms to refinery labs and drilling sites — TOPECH instruments and additives cover diverse oil product testing and lubrication needs.

🛢️

Lubricant Anti-Wear Demonstration

Side-by-side friction tests of ordinary vs. anti-wear oils. Ordinary oil locks the machine at 3–4 weights while anti-friction oil runs past 12 weights — a powerful visual sales tool.

Recommended: Portable Oil Friction Tester / Five-Digit Anti-Wear Tester
🏭

Petroleum Product Quality Control

Flash point, kinematic viscosity, pour point, density and color testing of fuels and lubricants according to ASTM / GB / ISO methods in refinery and blending QC labs.

Recommended: Flash Point Tester / LYV-8 / LY-445 / Pour Point Tester
⚡

Transformer & Turbine Oil Monitoring

Karl Fischer water content (ppm level) and acid value testing of insulating and turbine oils — the key condition indicators for power equipment reliability.

Recommended: LY-2100 Karl Fischer / Oil Acidity Analyzer
✈️

Fuel Corrosiveness Testing

Copper strip corrosion rating of aviation gasoline, jet fuel, kerosene, diesel and LPG per ASTM D130 / GB/T 5096 / SH/T 0232 for specification release.

Recommended: ASTM D130 Tester / LY-172 LPG Corrosion Bath
🛠️

Engine Oil Condition Analysis

High-temperature screening of carbon deposit, boiling point, oxidation and acidity to spot poor or counterfeit engine oils before they damage engines.

Recommended: High Temperature Engine Oil Testing Machine
⛏️

Drilling Fluid Lubrication

Boride extreme-pressure lubricant reduces torque and drag by over 90% in large-displacement, high-angle and horizontal wells with stable thermal performance.

Recommended: LY-3010 Boride EP Drilling Lubricant
🚗

Automotive Aftermarket Additives

Nano graphene, fullerene and boron-based anti-wear additives plus oil system cleaners for fuel-economy, repair-type and car-care product lines.

Recommended: Nano Graphene / Organic Fullerene / Engine Oil System Cleaner
🎓

Training & Research Laboratories

Clear digital readouts, parallel sample positions and standard-compliant methods make TOPECH instruments suitable for tribology teaching and research work.

Recommended: LYV-8 / Five-Digit Anti-Wear Tester
📦

OEM / Distributor Programs

Custom labeling, packaging and voltage configuration for distributors building their own instrument or additive brand with factory-direct support.
