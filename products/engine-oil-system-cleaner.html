Engine Oil System Cleaner

300 ml · Treats up to 5 L Oil · 15-Minute Idle Flush
This agent dissolves and removes carbon deposits, varnish-tar deposits and other contaminants on cylinder-piston group parts, releasing coked piston rings and stuck hydraulic lifters while neutralizing oil oxidation. High-performance lubricating agents protect the entire oil system during cleaning. Benefits: fresh oil in a clean engine, better fuel economy, increased output, exhaust emission compliance and extended catalyst life. Suitable for 4-stroke and diesel engines, manual gearboxes and differentials.
