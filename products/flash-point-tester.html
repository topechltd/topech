Fully Automatic Open / Closed Cup Flash Point Tester

ASTM D92 / ASTM D93 · Electronic Ignition · Touch Screen · No Gas Source Needed
The automatic flash point analyzer (open cup / closed cup) is designed in accordance with ASTM D92 / ASTM D93, ISO 2592, GB 267, GB 3536 and equivalent standards. Microcomputer technology makes stirring, ignition, testing, alerting, cooling and printing completely automatic. A built-in atmospheric pressure detector automatically corrects the flash point result, while the security airless platinum-alloy igniter removes the need for any gas source. Ideal for petroleum, chemical, power station, environmental, railway and scientific research laboratories.
