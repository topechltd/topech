Nano Graphene Lubricant Additive

Engine Oil · Wind Power Oil · Grease · Recommended Dosage 3%
The nano graphene additive is used in engine oil, wind power oil, mechanical oil, emulsion, drilling fluid and grease extreme-pressure lubricants. It has good oil solubility (I, II, III class base oils and PAO) with excellent dispersity, delivering anti-wear, anti-friction and anti-oxidation effects plus increased torque grade. Added to engine oil it also helps repairing, energy saving and relieving oil burning.
