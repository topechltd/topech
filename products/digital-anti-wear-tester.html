Anti-Wear Tester with Five-Digit Display

Displays V · A · W · kWh · Oil Temperature in Real Time
The new generation anti-wear tester adds five digital displays showing voltage (V), current (A), instantaneous power (W), power consumption (kWh) and instantaneous oil temperature (T). Run two machines side by side for a strictly fair comparison: place the same weights, run 3–5 minutes, and observe current, power and temperature differences. Lower oil temperature means better viscosity stability; lower power consumption means better fuel economy; stable color and no smoke indicate superior lubricant quality.
