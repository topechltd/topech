ASTM D130 Copper Strip Corrosion Test Machine

ASTM D130 · GB/T 5096 · Fuels & Lubricants Corrosion Assessment
This copper strip corrosion test machine complies with GB/T 5096 and ASTM D130, applicable to assessing the corrosion degree of aviation gasoline, jet fuel, motor gasoline, kerosene, diesel, natural gasoline, lubricants and other petroleum products on copper. Polished copper strips are immersed in the sample under controlled temperature, then compared against the ASTM copper strip corrosion classification standards to rate corrosiveness.
