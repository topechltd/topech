Lubricant Anti-Wear Performance Tester with Temperature Display

Oil Temperature Display · Weight Loading · Anti-Wear Comparison
The lubricant anti-wear performance tester combines the Timken OK load test with live oil temperature monitoring. During loading, the digital temperature display tracks how the lubricant behaves under friction — anti-wear oils stay cooler while poor oils heat up fast. Together with the stacked weight loading system, this gives two independent proofs of lubricant quality in a single 3–5 minute run: OK load value and temperature rise. A compact green cabinet, silver control panel and precision lever make it suitable for showrooms, labs and training centers.
